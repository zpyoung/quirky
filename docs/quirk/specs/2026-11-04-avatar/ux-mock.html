Where the avatar lives
  • Window bar pill: Avatar · 4 handed off, next to the needs-you count. Click it for the hand-off sheet or the report.
  • Stripe icon and Avatar panel (left-bottom slot by default): handed-off worktrees with state and level, plus Hand off… and Take back all.
  • Worktree sidebar: handed-off rows get an accent ring on the status dot and an Avatar · prefix.
  • Avatar view (center tab) with Live, Report and Log.
  • Hand-off sheet, in the shared popup shape. It opens from the panel, the pill, a worktree's context menu, or a sidebar multi-select.
  • Settings → Avatar for defaults, and quirky avatar … on the CLI.

Names, numbers and timestamps are illustrative. Styling follows the PyCharm-shell golden screens; a real build would use the shared graphite components and tokens.
